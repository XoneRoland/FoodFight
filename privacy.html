Privacy & guest play

You can play without registering

Guest registration is entirely optional. Choose Play without registering to play without sending a registration. Leaving consent unchecked does not prevent you from playing. Please choose a nickname, not your real name, if you decide to register.

What an optional registration sends

Only your chosen nickname and your checked consent choice are submitted to the EzyPlay administrator through Netlify Forms. We do not ask for your email, password, first or last name, or add device identifiers, location, game progress or tracking information to the form. We do not sell registrations or use them to create accounts or marketing lists.

Netlify, the hosting and forms provider, may process ordinary connection information needed to deliver and protect its service. Its privacy policy explains its processing. The game adds no fingerprinting or registration tracking scripts.

Who can view registrations

The EzyPlay administrator can view them in Netlify’s signed-in Forms dashboard. They are not published on this website. The game’s local admin password is a prototype screen control, not secure authentication. This website does not fetch private submissions.

Retention and removal

The administrator’s retention policy is to review submissions weekly and delete them within 30 days, or sooner once they are no longer needed. This is a manual responsibility in Netlify; the static website does not automatically delete server records. Unneeded exports, spam entries and notification copies should also be removed.

You can ask the EzyPlay administrator to remove a registration using your existing contact channel; quote only the nickname. Do not send identity documents or extra personal details. If you cannot contact the administrator or prefer not to share a nickname, choose Play without registering.

What stays on this device

A nickname used for the current play session stays in memory until reload or End Session. Registration details and consent are not written to localStorage by the game. Food Fight and Racer continue to keep their existing game progress locally in this browser/site. Clearing browser site data can remove that progress.

If a submission fails

There are no automatic retries. A failed or interrupted response may mean delivery is uncertain. To avoid repeated entries, only one registration attempt is made per guest session. You can continue playing without another submission. Ending the session begins a new voluntary choice; this is not a cross-device duplicate-prevention system.
